
# Title: Vehicle Crashes in Washington, D.C.  
### Author: Elijah Gardner Woods

**Created:** Sep 18, 2025  
**Last Updated:** Oct 17, 2025 -- by Elijah Gardner Woods  
**Dependencies:** Leaflet 1.9  

**Description:** This interactive webmap shows a sample of 686 vehicle crashes in Washington, D.C., between the years of 2010–2021. Most of the recorded crashes occurred between 2010-2014. The map divides the data by month, and allows users to move from month to month to see the crashes that occured in each month.  The crash data comes from Data.gov, and the data was retrieved Sep 28, 2025.
